## Outcome

Deployed an AI candidate pre-screening and interview scheduling assistant for a 200-person tech recruiting firm. Automated initial resume parsing (PDF/Word), technical questionnaire evaluation, and self-service interview booking, reviewing 8,000+ resumes and booking 950+ qualified technical screens with zero scheduling conflicts.

## Timeline

Delivered production MVP in 6 weeks with Next.js candidate portal, LayoutLM resume extraction pipeline, two-way Greenhouse and Lever ATS webhook integration, and Calendly/Google Calendar scheduling sync.
