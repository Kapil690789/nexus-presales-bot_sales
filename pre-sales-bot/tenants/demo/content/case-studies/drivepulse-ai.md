## Outcome

Built an automated 24/7 showroom lead assistant for an 8-rooftop automotive dealer group. Qualified inbound shoppers by budget and vehicle preference, scheduled 2,100+ verified test drive appointments, processed trade-in valuations with NHTSA VIN decoding, and delivered 3.4x faster lead response times.

## Timeline

Delivered production MVP in 6 weeks with Next.js dealership showroom widget, NHTSA VIN specs pipeline, DealerSocket and Reynolds & Reynolds DMS integration, and automated SMS confirmation reminders via Twilio.
