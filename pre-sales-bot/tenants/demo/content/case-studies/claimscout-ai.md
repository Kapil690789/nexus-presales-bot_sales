Automated First Notice of Loss (FNOL) claims processing and damage severity triage for a regional casualty insurer. Reduced initial claim submission time from 45 minutes to 6 minutes, processing 3,200+ monthly auto and property claims with automated policy deductible validation and instant damage photo AI inspection.

## Timeline

Delivered production MVP in 8 weeks featuring Next.js web portal, Flutter mobile photo uploader, Gemini Vision damage classification pipeline, and encrypted S3 incident evidence vault with Guidewire sync.
