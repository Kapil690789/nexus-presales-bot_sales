## Outcome

Deployed a multi-lingual guest concierge and dining reservation assistant across 4 boutique hotel properties. The assistant handles 85% of guest dining, amenity, and local tour inquiries in 12 languages, booking 1,800+ direct restaurant reservations and increasing on-premise dining spend by 38%.

## Timeline

Delivered production MVP in 7 weeks featuring Next.js web guest companion, WhatsApp Business Cloud API integration, OpenTable and Toast POS table locks, and Opera PMS guest folio synchronization.
