Frequently Asked Questions

How are your indicative estimates calculated?

Our advisor analyzes your platforms, complexity multipliers, database requirements, and team mix using deterministic formulas derived from over 40+ completed builds. Our technical leads confirm the scope in a 20-minute call.

Yes. You receive 100% intellectual property and full codebase ownership upon sprint milestone completion. We do not use proprietary vendor lock-in frameworks.

Yes. You can accept our standard mutual confidentiality terms directly inside the chat advisor or upload your custom NDA document for our legal team to countersign.

What is your sprint delivery cadence?

We work in 2-week agile sprints with weekly staging releases, asynchronous video walkthroughs, and direct Slack communication with your named delivery lead.
