Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Northline.

How accurate are the AI Advisor's indicative estimates?

Our advisor uses deterministic complexity formulas derived from over 40+ completed software projects. It factors in your platforms, integrations, authentication, and team mix to produce high-confidence indicative bands, which our technical leads confirm in a short discovery call.

Who owns the intellectual property and source code?

You own 100% of all intellectual property, source code, designs, and architectural assets upon payment. We do not use proprietary vendor lock-in frameworks.

Can we sign an NDA before sharing confidential specs?

Yes. You can accept our standard mutual confidentiality terms directly inside the chat advisor or upload your custom NDA document for our legal team to countersign.

What is your sprint delivery cadence?

We work in 2-week agile sprints with weekly staging releases, asynchronous video walkthroughs, and direct Slack communication with your named delivery lead.
