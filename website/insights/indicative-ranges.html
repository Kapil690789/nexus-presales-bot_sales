Indicative ranges vs quotes

The advisor’s number is a low-side range you can take to stakeholders. A strategist confirms the real commercials — and this demo’s numbers are dummy data.

A quote implies locked scope, a team mix, and terms. A chat does not have that. We still share a range so you can see whether a conversation is worth anyone’s time.

Why the first number sits low

The range sits below a typical close so the conversation can start. It is not a promise we will deliver the full product at that number, and it is not a discount on the same scope.

What a strategist confirms

Scope, commercial terms, and timeline. We will not commit to a fixed go-live date from chat, and we will not call the advisor’s range a final quote. Read how we price or open the advisor from contact.
