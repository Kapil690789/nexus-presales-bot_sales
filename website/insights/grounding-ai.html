Grounding an AI product in your documents

An assistant that cannot point at a source will invent one. We start with what it should do for the user, then what it is allowed to read.

Atlas cut first-draft RFP time from three days to four hours because the corpus was approved, chunked, and cited — not because we asked a model to “be a consultant.”

Ask two questions first

  • What should the assistant actually do for the user?
  • Do you already have documents or systems it should read?

If the answer to the second is “not yet,” the MVP is often retrieval and a review UI, not an agent that emails customers.
