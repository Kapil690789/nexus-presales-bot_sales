Harvest — farm-to-buyer marketplace

A two-sided MVP so growers could list harvests and buyers could purchase in one corridor — without rebuilding the whole market overnight.

iOS Android Flutter Firebase
Challenge

Growers and buyers were coordinating over chat and spreadsheets. Listings went stale and payments sat off-platform.

Approach

Flutter + Firebase for both sides: grower listings, buyer discovery, and a first-pass payment flow in one corridor.

Outcome

Launched a two-sided MVP in 16 weeks; first 1,200 listings in 90 days.
