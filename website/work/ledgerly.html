Ledgerly — multi-entity finance ops

A role-based ops console that replaced three workbooks so accountants could close entities without a full ERP programme.

Web Next.js PostgreSQL Auth
Challenge

Forty accountants reconciled three spreadsheet workbooks across entities. Errors showed up at close, not during the week.

Approach

Next.js and PostgreSQL with role-based access, a single close workflow, and the one customer-facing journey that mattered for ops.

Outcome

Replaced three spreadsheets with a role-based ops console for 40 accountants.
