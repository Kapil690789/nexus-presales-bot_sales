Privacy Policy & Security Standards

How Nexus processes, secures, and protects technical specifications, project briefs, and consultation data.

1. Technical Information Collected

When you interact with Mike (our AI advisor) or book a consultation, we collect technical requirements, project timelines, estimated budget thresholds, and optional architectural documentation you provide so we can formulate an accurate engineering scope and estimate.

2. Strict Confidentiality & Mutual NDA

All proprietary information, workflow architectures, business logic, and code snippets shared during scoping discussions are strictly treated as confidential. We execute standard bilateral NDAs prior to accessing private client codebases or proprietary datasets.

3. Security & Data Isolation

We adhere to enterprise security standards including AES-256 data-at-rest encryption and TLS 1.3 in-transit protection. Uploaded documentation is processed strictly in isolated memory and never used to train public foundation models without explicit authorization.

4. Inquiries & Data Rights

You may request deletion or export of your consultation transcript and session data at any time by contacting our engineering privacy team.
