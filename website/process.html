Delivery

How an engagement starts

We protect the budget by cutting phase-two scope, not by promising a date we cannot own. The advisor is the first pass; a strategist confirms the real number.

01 — Discover

Shape the product, not the brochure

Who is the first user, what must ship, and what can wait. Out-of-scope work is closed politely rather than estimated.

02 — Cut an MVP

One journey, an approved stack

Mobile, web, AI, or design — we recommend from stacks we actually deliver, then drop features that belong in phase two.

03 — Indicative range

A number you can take to stakeholders

The advisor shares a low-side range based on what you have shared. It is dummy data for this demo, and never a formal quote.

04 — Consultation

Named lead, real commercials

A DevConsult strategist confirms scope, terms, and timeline. We do not guarantee a go-live date from the chat.
