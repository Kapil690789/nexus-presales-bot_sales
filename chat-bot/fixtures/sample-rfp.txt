Project: Acme Ops Console (web application)

Goal:
Build a web-based operations console for mid-market distributors so account managers can track orders, inventory alerts, and customer notes in one place.

Users:
Internal account managers and a small ops leadership team (about 40 people at launch).

Platforms:
Web (desktop first). Mobile browser support is nice-to-have, not required for MVP.

Must-have MVP features:
- Email/password auth with role-based access (manager vs admin)
- Order list + order detail
- Inventory threshold alerts
- Customer note timeline
- Basic admin to invite users

Integrations:
- Salesforce (read contacts and accounts)
- Stripe (view payment status only)

Timeline:
We want a first usable release in 1–3 months.

Budget:
Exploring a $40–80k band for the MVP.

Decision role:
I am the Head of Operations / product owner for this initiative.

Constraints:
No custom mobile apps in phase one. Keep the stack boring and maintainable (Next.js + PostgreSQL preferred).
