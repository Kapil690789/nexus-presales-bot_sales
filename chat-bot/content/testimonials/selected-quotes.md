## On scoping and discovery

"We came in with a twenty-page spec and left discovery with a five-page one. The five
pages were the right ones." — Head of Product, Harvest (approved for use, 2025-11-04)

"You told us in week two that messaging would double the project and would not move the
number we cared about. You were right, and we shipped a quarter earlier for it."
— Director of Operations, Riverview (approved for use, name of organisation withheld,
2025-09-22)

## On the work itself

"The spreadsheets were never the problem. Not knowing who changed what was the problem,
and that is what this fixed." — Financial Controller, Ledgerly (approved for use,
2025-12-15)

"It does not tell us what we want to hear. When we have never answered something, it says
we have never answered it. That is why we use it." — Bid Director, Atlas engagement
(approved for use, 2026-01-08)

## On being told things straight

"We asked for a redesign and got told our onboarding asked for a database password before
it had earned one. Nobody internally had said it that bluntly." — VP Product, Northstar
(approved for use, 2026-01-20)

## On handover

"Four weeks after launch our own team owned the codebase, which is exactly what we were
told would happen at kickoff." — Engineering Manager, Riverview (approved for use, name of
organisation withheld, 2025-10-30)

## A note for whoever maintains this file

Every quote here has been approved in writing by the person quoted, and the approval date
is recorded next to it. Do not add a quote without that approval, and do not attach a
client's name where the approval covered only an anonymised attribution. Where a client
declined to be named, the role and the approval stay and the organisation comes out.
