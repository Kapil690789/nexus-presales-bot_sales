## What this covers

Product design as its own engagement: research, information architecture, interaction
and interface design, prototypes for validation, and design systems. Either ahead of a
build, alongside your engineering team, or as a standalone piece of work when you know
something is wrong but not yet what.

## The shapes this takes

- **Design ahead of a build.** Two to four weeks framing the product and designing the
  first release before engineering ramps. Reduces rework and gives everyone something
  concrete to argue about early, which is much cheaper than arguing about it later.
- **Prototype and validate.** A clickable prototype and a small round of user sessions,
  to test whether the idea holds before committing to a build budget. The cheapest
  useful thing we sell.
- **Fix a specific number.** Activation, onboarding, conversion, or task completion.
  Starts with watching real users and finding where they stall. Northstar is the
  example: eleven sessions found the problem, and it was not the one anyone expected.
- **Design system.** Component library, tokens, and documentation, built to match the
  code your team actually ships rather than an idealised version of it.

## How we work

We watch people use things. Not many people — five to eleven sessions finds the large
problems, and the large problems are where the value is. Opinions in a room, however
senior, are not a substitute, and one recorded session ends most internal debates.

Deliverables are Figma files organised so engineers can work from them: flows, states
including empty and error states, responsive behaviour, and a component set. We include
the states because the interesting bugs live in the states nobody designed.

## What we include

Research and synthesis, problem framing, flows and high-fidelity screens for the agreed
scope, component foundations, a validation round where the shape calls for it, and a
prioritised sequence for building it.

## What this is not

- We do not do brand identity, logo design, or marketing campaign creative.
- We do not do illustration or motion design as standalone work.
- We do not deliver screens without engineering context. If a design cannot be built by
  the team that has to build it, it is not finished.
- We do not run research where the outcome has already been decided. If the redesign is
  happening regardless, say so, and we will design instead of researching.
